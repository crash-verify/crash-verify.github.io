CrashTop round verifier

Every round publishes a hash before betting closes and reveals its server seed after the bust. This page rebuilds the hash chain from that hash and replays the results, entirely in your browser. It has no database, no login, and no connection to the game server.

How to check a round

  1. Open Round info in the game and copy the round hash and the round number.
  2. Paste them below, or press Verify hash in the game — it opens this page already filled in.
  3. Compare the rows with the results the game showed. A green verdict means the hash walks back to the chain anchor that was published before the chain was used.
